# Raptors Auto Group: Questions for the Bank

Milestone 0 · Version 1 · Linked to Decision 3 (bank for USD wires) and Decision 13 (bank details on invoices).

**Purpose.** At launch, international bank transfer (USD wire) to Raptors' US business account is the only way to pay. Before we build invoices, payment recording and receipts (Milestone 8), we need the bank's own answers to the questions below. Please ask the bank in writing, and keep its written reply.

## 1. Receiving international wires

1. Does the account accept incoming international SWIFT wires in USD from overseas customers?
2. Can the account receive wires in any currency other than USD? If a customer's bank sends another currency, what happens (is it converted, and at what rate and cost)?
3. What fee does the bank charge for each incoming international wire?
4. Intermediary (correspondent) banks may deduct fees before the money arrives. How do these deductions show on the statement, so Raptors can see the amount sent and the amount received?
5. Is there a different process or fee for wires from specific countries or regions (for example West Africa, the Middle East, the Caribbean)?

## 2. Limits and reviews

6. Is there a maximum amount for a single incoming wire, or a daily or monthly limit?
7. Above what amount does the bank review or hold an incoming wire, and how long can such a review take?
8. Will the bank ask Raptors for documents (for example an invoice or a customer's identity) before releasing a large incoming wire?
9. Are there countries from which the bank will not accept wires?

## 3. Clearing and confirmation

10. How long after the customer sends a wire do the funds usually reach the account and become available?
11. How does Raptors confirm that funds have **fully cleared** and cannot be pulled back (statement entry, online banking status, notification)? The platform will only record a payment after Raptors has confirmed this in its own bank.
12. Does the bank send a notification (email or app) when an incoming wire arrives?
13. Which details of the sender does the statement show (name, bank, reference text)?

## 4. Returned and recalled wires

14. If a wire arrives with wrong or incomplete details, what does the bank do (hold, return, ask Raptors)? How long does it take, and what does it cost?
15. Can the sending bank recall a wire after it has been credited to Raptors' account? Under what conditions, and for how long afterwards?
16. If a wire is returned, who pays the fees, and how much of the amount reaches the sender?

## 5. Overpayments and refunds

17. If a customer pays more than the invoice, what is the process to send the difference back? (Refunds are a new outgoing wire, never a reversal.)
18. What does an outgoing international wire cost, and how long does it take?
19. Can Raptors refuse to refund to a different account or person than the one that paid? (This protects against overpayment scams.)

## 6. Who can pay

20. Does the bank accept wires sent by a third party (for example a relative or company paying for the buyer), or only from the named customer?
21. If third-party payments are accepted, does the bank require anything extra?

## 7. Wire instructions and the payment reference

22. Does the bank provide **official wire instructions** for overseas senders (account name, account number, routing or ABA number, SWIFT or BIC code, bank name and address, any intermediary bank)? Can these be printed on Raptors' invoices?
23. Is an intermediary bank required for some currencies or countries? If so, its details.
24. Which field should customers use for Raptors' **payment reference** (for example the invoice number)? How many characters does it allow, and which characters are accepted? Is the reference reliably shown on Raptors' statement?

## 8. Fraud and security

25. How does the bank recommend Raptors protects customers against fake wire instructions (for example emails impersonating Raptors with different bank details)?
26. If Raptors' bank details ever change, what does the bank recommend for telling customers safely?
