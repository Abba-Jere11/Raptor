# Raptors Auto Group: What We Need From You

Milestone 0 · Version 1 · For the client (CEO of Raptors Auto Group).

Please supply each item below, or tell us it is not available yet. Tick each box when it has been sent. The decision number in brackets links to the Decisions Register (`01_decisions_register.md`). Items marked **Before M1** are needed before design work starts.

---

## 1. Business identity and licence (Decision 2) · Before M1

- [ ] Full legal name of the company, as registered
- [ ] Trading name, if different ("Raptors Auto Group")
- [ ] State of registration and company registration number
- [ ] Dealer licence number, issuing state and authority, and expiry date
- [ ] Business address to publish on the website and on documents
- [ ] Business phone number and general email address to publish
- [ ] Year the business started, and any figures you are happy to publish (for example, countries shipped to). Only real, recorded figures are shown.
- [ ] Named contact person(s) to show to customers, with a photo if you wish

## 2. Brand assets (Decision 8) · Before M1

- [ ] **Final logo file** in vector format (SVG, AI or PDF), with a light and a dark version
- [ ] The interim cleaned logo vectors (`logo_clean/`) referred to in the plan, if you still have them
- [ ] Any brand guidelines, fonts or colour notes beyond the approved palette
- [ ] Photography capacity: who photographs the vehicles, with what equipment, and how many vehicles a week
- [ ] Video capacity: whether walk-around videos will be filmed, and by whom
- [ ] 360° capacity: whether a 24 to 36 frame turntable or walk-around sequence can be shot
- [ ] Sample real photographs of 2 or 3 vehicles (needed for the client to approve the look in Milestone 1)

## 3. Domain and web addresses (Decision 8) · Before M1

- [ ] The domain name to use (for example `raptorsauto.com`), and whether you already own it
- [ ] Who holds the domain today, and login access for the developer (or the developer is added as a user)
- [ ] Journey Tracker address: a `track.` sub-domain (for example `track.raptorsauto.com`) or a page on the main site (`/track`)
- [ ] The email address customer emails should come from (for example `quotes@` or `hello@`)

## 4. Bank details for USD wires (Decision 3) · Before M8, start now

- [ ] Name of the bank for receiving USD wires
- [ ] The bank's answers to the questions in `03_bank_questions.md`
- [ ] Incoming international wire fees charged by the bank
- [ ] Whether the bank issues official wire instructions that can be given to overseas customers
- [ ] The exact wire instructions to print on invoices (account name, account number, routing or ABA number, SWIFT code, bank address). Supply these only through a secure channel, never by WhatsApp or plain email.

## 5. Destinations (Decision 1) · Before M7

- [ ] The full list of countries Raptors already ships to
- [ ] The **3 to 5 first-priority countries** to launch with
- [ ] For each, the destination port(s) and the method used (RoRo, container, or both)
- [ ] Anything Raptors has confirmed recently about each destination's rules, with the date. These are stored "as last confirmed on [date]", never stated as fact.

## 6. Freight forwarders and shipping lines (Decision 4) · Before M7

- [ ] Name and contact details of each freight forwarder Raptors uses
- [ ] Which forwarder handles export filing (the platform only records status; the forwarder files)
- [ ] Shipping lines used, and the US ports Raptors ships from

## 7. Platforms to name on the website (Decision 10) · Before M5

- [ ] The full list of platforms Raptors is a registered dealer on (Manheim, OpenLane, and others)
- [ ] Whether official platform logos may be shown (permission from each platform, if required)
- [ ] Confirmation that no account numbers, lots, supplier links or wholesale prices will be shown (this is the plan's rule)

## 8. WhatsApp and languages (Decision 9) · Before M1

- [ ] The WhatsApp number to use for click-to-chat
- [ ] Whether WhatsApp stays click-to-chat only, or automation is wanted later (automation is not in release 1)
- [ ] Website language(s) at launch (release 1 plans English only; a multi-language site is listed for later)

## 9. Start now for later milestones

These block later milestones, but involve outside parties, so it helps to start them now.

- [ ] **Fee schedule** for own-stock sales and vehicle requests (Decision 5, before M6)
- [ ] **Invoice and receipt details agreed with Raptors' accountant**: numbering, tax lines, terms and the bank details to print (Decision 13, before M6)

## Sending items securely

- Logos, photos and documents: a shared folder (for example Google Drive or Dropbox) in Raptors' own account, shared with the developer.
- Bank details and licence documents: send through a secure channel agreed with the developer, never by WhatsApp or unencrypted email attachment.
- Never send any Manheim, OpenLane or other platform passwords. The platform does not need them and will not store them.
