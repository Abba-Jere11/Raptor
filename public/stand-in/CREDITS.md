## Older renders still in this folder

Remaining `car-*` and `gallery-*` files (except the three above) are renders of one 3D model, repainted, extracted from the Master Plan v5 PDF with `scripts/extract-standins.mjs`.

- Model: "Ferrari 458 Italia" by **vicent091036** (Sketchfab), licensed **Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)**, distributed with the three.js examples, rendered with three.js (Master Plan v5, Section 23).
- The Ferrari badge on the model should be hidden or replaced in final designs if the client prefers (Master Prompt, Part 4, item 5).
